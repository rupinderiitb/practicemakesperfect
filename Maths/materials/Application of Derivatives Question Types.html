JEE Main Maths – Application of Derivatives

Types of questions asked in JEE Main 2019–2025 (183 questions)

Key message: these 183 questions fall into only 9 repeating types. The three biggest types (Types 1, 2, 3) account for 107 questions (58%). Maxima and minima alone (Type 1) are 44 questions (24%). About 23% of the questions ask for a numerical (integer) answer, so there are no options to guess from.

How to read this: a “type” is the formula, law or technique that decides the answer. So every type below tells you what the question looks like and which formula or method to use.

3. Type-by-type detail

Type 1: Maxima and minima: local and absolute extrema – 44 questions

What is asked: Find the points of local maxima or minima of a function, the value at them, the absolute maximum and minimum on an interval, or the parameter for which a function has given extrema.

What to master: First derivative test; second derivative test; critical points where f′ does not exist; comparing end-point values; splitting |…| and [x] functions; treating a sequence as a function of a real variable.

Forms that appear: f(x) = (3x² + ax − 2 − a)eˣ with critical point x = 1, f(x) = ||x + 2| − 2|x||, f(x) = x³ − 3x² − …, sum of absolute maximum and minimum of |x² − 5x + 6| − 3x + 2 on [−1, 3], f(x) = tan⁻¹(sin x − cos x), greatest term of an = n³/(n⁴ + 147).

Asked in: 2019 (4), 2020 (2), 2021 (8), 2022 (9), 2023 (8), 2024 (8), 2025 (5)

Which form was asked:

Local maxima and minima and critical points of a given function: 28 questions

Absolute maximum and minimum on a closed interval: 13 questions

Range of a function, greatest term, least parameter value: 3 questions

Type 2: Tangent and normal to a curve – 32 questions

What is asked: Find the equation of a tangent or normal, a point that lies on it, or use a condition (parallel, perpendicular, passing through a point, angle between curves, area of the triangle formed) to find a parameter.

What to master: Slope as dy/dx; tangent y − y₁ = m(x − x₁); normal with slope −1/m; parametric and implicit differentiation; angle between curves; tangent from a given point.

Forms that appear: tangent to y = x³ + 3x² + 5 through the origin, normal to y = (1+x)^(2y) + cos²(sin⁻¹x) at x = 0, curves x = y⁴ and xy = k cutting at right angles, area of a triangle formed by the tangent and normal to a circle, tangent to y = eˣ and normal to y² = 4x meeting on the x-axis.

Asked in: 2019 (5), 2020 (8), 2021 (7), 2022 (7), 2023 (5)

Which form was asked:

Equation of a tangent or normal and points on it: 13 questions

Tangent or normal with a condition: parallel, perpendicular, common, angle, area or fixed point: 19 questions

Type 3: Monotonicity: increasing and decreasing functions – 31 questions

What is asked: Find the intervals where a function increases or decreases, or the parameter (a, λ) for which it is increasing or decreasing on an interval.

What to master: Sign of f′(x); f′(x) ≥ 0 for all x (use the discriminant or the extreme value of f′); intervals for |x|, piecewise and logarithmic functions; compositions and a function g(x) = f(x) + f(c − x).

Forms that appear: f(x) = xˣ increasing, f(x) = x e^(x(1−x)), f(x) = 4λx³ − 36λx² + 36x + 48 increasing for all x, f = e^x − x composed with x² − x, f(x) = 3 log_e|(x − 1)/(x + 1)| − 2/(x − 1).

Asked in: 2019 (4), 2020 (3), 2021 (7), 2022 (5), 2023 (2), 2024 (8), 2025 (2)

Type 4: Rolle's theorem and Lagrange's mean value theorem – 18 questions

What is asked: Find the value c given by the theorem, check its conditions, or use it on an abstract function to count zeros or to bound values.

What to master: Rolle: f(a) = f(b) ⇒ f′(c) = 0; LMVT: f′(c) = (f(b) − f(a))/(b − a); repeated Rolle for f′ and f″; bounds from f′(x) ≤ k; convex and concave functions.

Forms that appear: f(x) = x³ − 4x² + 8x + 11 on [0, 1], x log x with c parallel to the chord, Rolle for x³ − ax² + bx − 4 on [1, 2], minimum number of zeros of f″ for f(0) = 3, f(1) = 5, ratio (f(c) − f(a))/(f(b) − f(c)) for f′ > 0, f″ < 0.

Asked in: 2019 (1), 2020 (9), 2021 (4), 2022 (2), 2023 (1), 2024 (1)

Which form was asked:

Rolle's theorem and Lagrange's mean value theorem (direct): 7 questions

Mean value theorem on abstract functions and counting zeros: 11 questions

Type 5: Geometrical optimisation: maximum area and volume – 17 questions

What is asked: Maximise or minimise an area, volume or surface area of a figure that is inscribed in another figure or built from a given length of wire or sheet.

What to master: Choose one variable; express the quantity using the constraint; set the derivative equal to zero; check the nature of the point.

Forms that appear: largest rectangle in y = x² − 1 below the x-axis, cylinder of maximum volume in a sphere of radius 3, open box from a rectangular sheet, wire cut into a square and a circle, rectangle in an equilateral triangle, cone of slant height 3.

Asked in: 2019 (3), 2020 (1), 2021 (5), 2022 (3), 2023 (3), 2024 (2)

Which form was asked:

Maximum area or volume of an inscribed figure; box and cone problems: 12 questions

Cut-wire and constant-sum problems: 5 questions

Type 6: Number of real roots of an equation – 12 questions

What is asked: Find how many real roots an equation has, or the range of a parameter for which it has the given number of roots.

What to master: Define f(x) from the equation; local maximum and minimum values and their signs; monotonic function crosses the axis at most once; Rolle's theorem; limits at ±∞.

Forms that appear: x⁷ − 7x − 2 = 0, x⁴ − 4x + 1 = 0, 3x⁴ + 4x³ − 12x² + 4 = 0, e⁴ˣ + 2e³ˣ − eˣ − 6 = 0, 5x³ − 15x − a = 0 with three distinct real roots.

Asked in: 2021 (4), 2022 (4), 2023 (2), 2025 (2)

Type 7: Polynomials determined by extremum conditions – 10 questions

What is asked: Find the polynomial (or its value at a point) from the given maximum or minimum values and critical points, and then use it.

What to master: Writing f′ using its known roots; using f(a) = value at a critical point; behaviour near 0 from lim f(x)/xⁿ; deducing symmetry or odd and even structure.

Forms that appear: cubic with local minima at x = 1 and f′ having a local minimum at x = −1, degree-6 polynomial with extrema at ±1 and lim f/x³ = 1, degree-4 polynomial with critical points at −1, 0, 1 and the set f(x) = f(0).

Asked in: 2019 (1), 2020 (4), 2021 (3), 2022 (1), 2025 (1)

Type 8: Shortest distance – 8 questions

What is asked: Find the shortest distance from a point or a line to a curve, or between two curves, or the minimum of a sum of squared distances.

What to master: Distance squared as a function of one variable; nearest point where the tangent is parallel to the line; the normal at the nearest point passes through the given point.

Forms that appear: line x − y = 1 and curve x² = 2y, line y = x and curve y² = x − 2, point (3/2, 0) and curve y = √x, helicopter along y − x^(3/2) = 7, parabola y² = 8x and circle x² + y² + 12y + 35 = 0.

Asked in: 2019 (3), 2020 (1), 2021 (1), 2022 (1), 2024 (1), 2025 (1)

Which form was asked:

Shortest distance between a point or curve and a curve or line: 6 questions

Minimum of a sum of squared distances: 2 questions

Type 9: Rate of change: related rates – 8 questions

What is asked: A quantity changes with time; find the rate of change of another quantity at a given instant.

What to master: Relation between the quantities; differentiation with respect to time (chain rule); volumes and areas of sphere, cone and cube; a ladder against a wall.

Forms that appear: ice melting on a ball of radius 10 cm, water poured into a conical tank, surface area of a cube increasing at 3.6 cm²/s, ladder of 2 m sliding down a wall.

Asked in: 2019 (3), 2020 (2), 2022 (2), 2025 (1)

5. The few genuinely new questions

These do not fit a repeating pattern. See each once; they do not need heavy practice.

f and g defined through each other with f′ and f″ of g in the definition; both are found by solving for g′(1), g″(2) (2023)

maximum of z = 6xy + y² under two linear constraints (extreme value in a region) (2021)

f(x) = 2x² − log x gives a from its monotonic intervals, then a tangent to the parabola y² = 4ax is needed (2022)

6. Suggested study order for students

Start with maxima and minima (Type 1): 44 questions (24%). Master the first and second derivative tests and absolute extrema on an interval.

Then tangent and normal and monotonicity (Types 2 and 3): 63 questions (34%).

Then the mean value theorems, optimisation, roots and polynomials (Types 4, 5, 6 and 7): 57 questions, and finally distance and rates (Types 8 and 9): 16 questions.

7. A routine to follow for every question

Read what is asked: a tangent or normal, an extremum, an interval of increase, or a number of roots, and note the point or interval given.

Find f′(x) first; almost every type begins with it. Factorise f′ and mark the sign of f′ on a number line.

Check the end points of the interval and the points where the function or f′ is not defined (modulus, [x], x^(2/3)).

For word problems write the quantity in one variable using the constraint before you differentiate.

For an abstract function (f″ > 0, f(0) = f(1) = 0, …) use Rolle or the mean value theorem and check the options with a simple example.
