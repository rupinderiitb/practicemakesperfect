3. Type-by-type detail

Type 1: Points of non-differentiability: modulus, max/min and greatest-integer functions – 28 questions

What is asked: A function built from |·|, max, min or [·] is given and the number (or set) of points where it is not differentiable is asked.

What to master: Corners where the left and right derivatives differ; |f| has a corner where f changes sign with f′ ≠ 0; max/min switches where the graphs cross; [x] terms give jumps at integers.

Forms that appear: the number of points where |x² − 2x − 3|e^{9x² − 12x + 4} is not differentiable, max{x, x²}, min{sin x, cos x}, f(x) = min{|x|, 2 − x²} with [|x|], |x − 1| + |x − 2| type sums, the set of points for f(f(x)) = 15 − |x − 10|.

Asked in: 2019 (6), 2020 (2), 2021 (7), 2022 (6), 2023 (2), 2024 (3), 2025 (2)

Which form was asked:

Modulus functions: points where f is not differentiable (including |f(x)| combinations): 10 questions

Maximum or minimum of functions: points of non-differentiability: 8 questions

Greatest integer and fractional-part terms: points of non-differentiability: 7 questions

Composite and piecewise combinations: count the non-differentiable points: 3 questions

Type 2: Continuity: finding constants by evaluating limits at the junction – 25 questions

What is asked: A piecewise function with unknown constants is continuous at a point; find the constants (or an expression in them) by evaluating the limits.

What to master: Left limit = right limit = f(a); sin x/x → 1, (1 − cos x)/x² → ½, log(1 + x)/x → 1, (e^x − 1)/x → 1, (1 + kx)^{1/x} → e^k.

Forms that appear: continuity at x = 0 of (sin(a + 2)x + sin x)/x, of (1 + sin x)^{3a/sin x}, (1 + cos x)^{λ/cos x}, ratios of radicals with x^{1/3}, 72^x − 9^x − 8^x + 1 over a cosine difference, find a + 2b or 6a + b².

Asked in: 2019 (2), 2020 (2), 2021 (9), 2022 (3), 2023 (1), 2024 (6), 2025 (2)

Which form was asked:

Continuity: constants from sin, tan, log and cos ratio limits: 18 questions

Continuity: constants from exponential, 1^∞ and radical limits: 7 questions

Type 3: Points of discontinuity: composite and greatest-integer functions – 19 questions

What is asked: Count the points where a function containing [·] (or a composition fog, gof) is discontinuous.

What to master: [g(x)] jumps where g(x) is an integer and g is not constant there; at a break point compare left and right limits and the value; a composite is discontinuous where the inner function hits a break point of the outer function.

Forms that appear: the number of points where [x²/2] − [√x] on [0, 4] is discontinuous, [4x² − 1] on (−1, 1) with modulus branches, [x] with sin(π/([x] + 3)), fog for [x] and |1 − x| with e^x − x.

Asked in: 2019 (2), 2020 (2), 2021 (2), 2022 (4), 2023 (4), 2024 (2), 2025 (3)

Type 4: Constants of a piecewise function from continuity or differentiability – 17 questions

What is asked: Determine the unknown constants of a piecewise function using continuity (and, when stated, differentiability) at the junctions, then evaluate a combination of them.

What to master: Continuity: equal values; differentiability: equal values and equal one-sided derivatives; use several junctions when there are three or more pieces.

Forms that appear: f = 1/x for x ≥ 1 and ax² + b below, differentiable on R, 48(a + b); x² + 3x + a and bx + 2, ∫ of f over [−2, 2]; ae^x + be^{−x}, cx², ax² + 2cx with f′(0) + f′(2) = e; g linear with a limit branch.

Asked in: 2019 (2), 2020 (3), 2021 (3), 2022 (3), 2023 (1), 2024 (4), 2025 (1)

Type 5: Testing continuity or differentiability at a point or on a set – 10 questions

What is asked: A function is given (piecewise, integral-defined or with [x]) and the correct statement about its continuity or differentiability is to be chosen.

What to master: Continuous at a if the limits match; differentiable at a if the limits of the difference quotient from both sides match; d/dx ∫ f = f; |x| is not differentiable at 0.

Forms that appear: x² sin(1/x) at 0, f(x) = ∫₀ˣ[y] dy, π/4 + tan⁻¹x or ½(|x| − 1) on R − {±1}, f continuous at 4 with an integral branch and which statement is NOT true, [x − 1]cos((2x − 1)π/2).

Asked in: 2019 (1), 2020 (1), 2021 (5), 2022 (2), 2023 (1)

Type 6: Functional equations and abstract functions – 7 questions

What is asked: A relation such as f(x + y) = f(x)f(y), an inequality on |f(x) − f(y)| or a differential-type condition is given; deduce f and evaluate an integral, limit or value.

What to master: f′(x) = lim (f(x + h) − f(x))/h with the functional equation; |f(x) − f(y)| ≤ k|x − y|^p, p > 1 ⇒ f′ = 0; differentiate integrals and limits.

Forms that appear: f(x + y) = f(x)f(y) with f′(0) = 3 and the limit of (f(h) − 1)/h, f(x/y) = f(x)/f(y) with f′(1) = 2024, |f(x) − f(y)| ≤ 2|x − y|^{3/2} and ∫₀¹ f²(x) dx, f″ = g″ + 6x.

Asked in: 2019 (1), 2021 (2), 2023 (1), 2024 (2), 2025 (1)

5. Suggested study order for students

Start with counting the points of non-differentiability and discontinuity (Types 1 and 3): 47 questions (44%). Master the corner test and the jump test for [x].

Then finding constants by limits and by matching pieces (Types 2 and 4): 42 questions (40%).

Then testing statements at a point and functional equations (Types 5 and 6): 17 questions.

6. A routine to follow for every question

Read what is asked: continuity, differentiability, the number of points, or the value of a constant.

Identify the break points: zeros of modulus expressions, switch points of max/min, integers where [·] jumps, and the junctions of the pieces.

At each break point compare the left and right limits (continuity) and then the left and right derivatives (differentiability).

Use the standard limits for the constants, and count only the points that really fail (check cancellations and jumps that are removed).

Check the answer by substituting the constants back and testing one point on each side of every break point.
