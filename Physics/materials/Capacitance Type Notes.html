How to use these notes

1. Do these pages BEFORE the practice sheet, one type at a time.

2. Close all books and notes. Fill the blanks from memory; leave a gap if you cannot recall.

3. Then press “Show completed notes” under that type, correct your answers in a different colour (or your own notes), and note what you missed.

4. Now do that type's questions on the sheet. Come back to the notes after the sheet to add anything new.

5. Re-fill the same page a week later with the prompts covered – this is what makes it stick.

Opens the print screen: choose “Save as PDF”. Only the types you have written in are included.

2. Formulae and laws

Capacitor with a slab

C=ε0Ad−t+t/K

Metal sheet of thickness t

C=ε0Ad−t

Layers in series

C=ε0A∑di/Ki

Side by side (areas Ai)

C=ε0d∑KiAi

Maximum voltage

Vmax=Emaxd

3. Concepts and skills

What does a metal sheet do to the capacitance?

It removes a length equal to its thickness from the gap (K→∞).

When are dielectrics in series and when in parallel?

Layers along the field are in series; layers across the plates, each over part of the area, are in parallel.

Does the position of the slab matter?

No, only its thickness t and the constant K enter the capacitance.

4. Mistakes to avoid

• Using t/K instead of d−t+t/K.

• Forgetting to convert mm, cm​2 and pF.

• Adding the capacitances of layers that are in series.

• Using the free charge instead of the voltage when the dielectric strength is the limit.

3. Concepts and skills

When does a capacitor drop out of a network?

When both of its ends are at the same potential (shorted or on a balanced bridge).

Which quantity is common in series?

The charge; the voltage divides in inverse proportion to C.

4. Mistakes to avoid

• Using the parallel rule for capacitors in series.

• Missing a shorting wire.

• Taking the same voltage on capacitors in series.

Identical capacitors, one uncharged

3. Concepts and skills

What is conserved?

The total charge on the plates that are joined, not the energy.

Where does the lost energy go?

Heat and radiation in the wires, whatever the resistance.

4. Mistakes to avoid

• Joining like plates when the problem joins opposite plates.

• Conserving energy instead of charge.

• Using the series formula for capacitors that are joined in parallel.

4. Mistakes to avoid

• Forgetting the work done by the field on the slab.
