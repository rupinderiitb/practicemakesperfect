3. Type-by-type detail

Type 1: Charged particle in a magnetic field: radius, period and force on a moving charge – 38 questions

What is asked: Find the radius, period or frequency of a charged particle moving at right angles to a uniform magnetic field, compare particles of different mass and charge, or find the magnetic force on a moving charge.

What to master: r = mv/(qB), T = 2πm/(qB), r in terms of kinetic energy, potential or momentum, F = q(v × B), and the fact that the magnetic force does no work.

Forms that appear: a proton and an alpha particle with the same velocity or the same kinetic energy: the ratio of radii; an electron accelerated through a potential difference: the radius; ions of two masses and charges with the same energy; the time to return to the original place; the force on a charge moving along y in a field along z.

Asked in: 2019 (3), 2020 (3), 2021 (6), 2022 (11), 2023 (3), 2024 (6), 2025 (6)

Which form was asked:

Circular motion in a magnetic field: radius, period and frequency, and ratios for different particles: 23 questions

Force on a moving charge: direction, vector form and statements: 15 questions

Type 2: Field of circular loops, arcs and coils – 32 questions

What is asked: Find the magnetic field at the centre or on the axis of a circular loop or coil, or of arcs and semicircles combined with straight wires.

What to master: B = μ₀NI/(2R) at the centre, the axial formula, B = μ₀Iθ/(4πR) for an arc, and the right-hand rule for direction.

Forms that appear: the centre of a coil of 100 turns; the ratio of the field at the centre and at a point on the axis; two coils in perpendicular planes; two semicircles of radii 2π m and 4π m; a wire bent into a loop with N turns and n turns; a rotating charged ring.

Asked in: 2019 (3), 2020 (2), 2021 (4), 2022 (6), 2023 (11), 2024 (3), 2025 (3)

Which form was asked:

Field of a circular loop or coil: at the centre and on the axis, turns and ratios: 18 questions

Arcs, semicircles and loops with straight leads: combining the fields: 14 questions

Type 3: Ampere's law: solenoid, wire cross-section and cables – 24 questions

What is asked: Use Ampere's law to find the field of a solenoid, inside and outside a current-carrying wire or cylinder, or in a coaxial cable; or judge statements on the Biot-Savart and Ampere laws.

What to master: B = μ₀nI (with μ_r for a core), B = μ₀Ir/(2πa²) inside a uniform wire, B = μ₀I/(2πr) outside, and the enclosed current in a coaxial cable.

Forms that appear: a 200-turn solenoid with a field of 2.9×10⁻⁴ T; the ratio of the fields at a/2 and 2a; the plot of B against r for a long wire; the field zero in a coaxial cable; a core of relative permeability 500; the half-maximum field inside and outside a wire.

Asked in: 2019 (1), 2020 (2), 2021 (5), 2022 (3), 2023 (4), 2024 (5), 2025 (4)

Which form was asked:

Solenoid: field, turns per length, core and flux: 10 questions

Ampere's law: field of a wire cross-section, hollow cylinder and coaxial cable: 10 questions

Biot-Savart law, Ampere's law and the field of the loop: statements and plots: 4 questions

Type 4: Force on current-carrying conductors and between parallel wires – 22 questions

What is asked: Find the force on a straight or bent wire in a magnetic field, the force between two long parallel wires, or the balance of a suspended wire.

What to master: F = BIL sin θ, F/l = μ₀I₁I₂/(2πd), the equivalent straight length of a bent wire, and the force on a loop near a long wire.

Forms that appear: a 4 cm wire with 8 A in a field of 0.15 T; a suspended wire balanced by a field; two long wires 5 cm apart, then the distance halved and the currents doubled; a triangular wire in a field; a square loop near a long wire.

Asked in: 2019 (3), 2020 (2), 2021 (1), 2022 (5), 2023 (5), 2024 (5), 2025 (1)

Which form was asked:

Force between parallel wires and on a wire near a current: 10 questions

Force on a straight wire, a bent wire or a loop in a uniform or varying field: 12 questions

Type 5: Charge in combined electric and magnetic fields, helix and cyclotron – 22 questions

What is asked: Find the field or velocity for undeflected motion in crossed fields, the pitch of a helical path, the energy gained in a cyclotron, or the path of a particle crossing a field region.

What to master: v = E/B for no deflection, the helix with v∥ and v⊥, pitch = 2πm v cos θ/(qB), and K = q²B²R²/(2m) for a cyclotron.

Forms that appear: a proton moving undeflected at 2×10⁵ m/s, then the electric field is switched off; an electron of 728 eV in a velocity selector; a proton entering the field at 60°: the pitch; a proton in a cyclotron of radius 60 cm and field 1 T; a particle entering a region of width d.

Asked in: 2019 (4), 2020 (8), 2021 (1), 2022 (2), 2023 (2), 2024 (2), 2025 (3)

Which form was asked:

Charge in crossed electric and magnetic fields: undeflected motion, velocity selector: 10 questions

Helical path: pitch and radius: 4 questions

Cyclotron, motion in a bounded field and other composite motion: 8 questions

Type 6: Magnetic moment and magnetic dipole – 19 questions

What is asked: Find the magnetic moment of a loop, a bent wire or a moving charge, or the torque, work, period and field of a bar magnet or a dipole.

What to master: M = NIA, M = IA for a circle of given wire length, τ = MB sin θ, W = MB(cos θ₁ − cos θ₂), T = 2π√(I/(MB)), and the axial and equatorial fields of a dipole.

Forms that appear: a wire of length 314 cm carrying 14 A bent into a circle; a square loop turned into a circular loop; a bar magnet of moment 5 A m² turned through 180° in 0.4 T; the ratio of moments from the time periods 3 s and 4 s; a dipole of moment 1 A m² at 1 m.

Asked in: 2019 (3), 2020 (4), 2021 (2), 2022 (4), 2023 (2), 2024 (3), 2025 (1)

Which form was asked:

Magnetic moment of a current loop or a charge in motion: 9 questions

Bar magnet and dipole in a field: torque, work, oscillation and field of a dipole: 10 questions

Type 7: Field of straight wires and of polygons – 17 questions

What is asked: Find the field of a finite straight segment, of two or more long wires, or at the centre of a triangle, square or hexagon carrying a current.

What to master: B = (μ₀I/(4πd))(sin α + sin β), B = μ₀I/(2πd) for a long wire, and the sum over the sides of a polygon.

Forms that appear: a segment of 6 cm carrying 5 A and a point at a given distance; two wires 5 cm apart with equal currents in opposite directions; a square loop of side 1/√2 m; an equilateral triangle of side 4√3 cm; a regular hexagon of 50 turns.

Asked in: 2019 (4), 2020 (1), 2021 (1), 2022 (2), 2023 (2), 2024 (4), 2025 (3)

Which form was asked:

Field at the centre of a polygon (triangle, square, hexagon): 8 questions

Field of straight wires and finite segments: one wire, two wires, perpendicular wires: 9 questions

Type 8: Torque on a current loop and the moving coil galvanometer – 12 questions

What is asked: Find the torque on a coil in a magnetic field, or the deflection, sensitivity, torsional constant or conversion of a moving coil galvanometer.

What to master: τ = NIAB sin θ, φ = NIAB/k, current sensitivity NAB/k, voltage sensitivity NAB/(kR), and the shunt for an ammeter.

Forms that appear: a triangle of side 10 cm in a horizontal field of 20 mT; a rectangular coil of 100 turns turned through 45°; a galvanometer with 100 turns of area 2 cm², a field 0.01 T and a deflection of 0.05 rad; the ways of raising the current sensitivity.

Asked in: 2019 (3), 2021 (1), 2022 (1), 2023 (2), 2024 (3), 2025 (2)

6. Suggested study order for students

Start with the two largest families: the charged particle in a field (Type 1, 38 questions, 20%) and the field of circular loops and arcs (Type 2, 32, 17%).

Then Ampere's law and solenoids (Type 3, 24 questions) and the forces on conductors (Type 4, 22).

Then the combined fields, helix and cyclotron (Type 5, 22) and the magnetic moment and dipole (Type 6, 19).

Finish with straight wires and polygons (Type 7, 17), the torque and galvanometer (Type 8, 12) and the one-off questions.

7. A routine to follow for every question

Decide first which family the question belongs to: a particle in a field, the field of a current (loop, wire, solenoid), the force on a conductor, or a magnetic moment and torque.

Draw the figure with the direction of the current and use the right-hand rule to fix the direction of the field or force before the magnitude.

For a particle write r = mv/(qB) and express the ratio in terms of m, q and the given quantity (K, V, p or v) before putting numbers.

For a combination of segments add the fields as vectors; a straight lead along a radial line contributes nothing at the centre of an arc.

For Ampere's law find the current enclosed by the loop first, then B = μ₀I_enc/(2πr).
