2. Formulae and laws

Faraday's law

ε=−NdΦdt

Flux

Induced current

I=εR

Charge that flows

q=NΔΦR

Heat

H=∫ε2Rdt

3. Concepts and skills

What decides the emf?

Only the rate of change of the flux, not the flux itself.

Which component of B⃗ counts?

The component along the normal of the loop: B⃗⋅A⃗.

Does the charge depend on the time taken?

No, q=NΔΦ/R depends only on the change of flux.

4. Mistakes to avoid

• Using the flux at the time instead of its derivative.

• Taking cosθ with the angle to the plane instead of to the normal.

• Forgetting the number of turns N.

• Not converting mWb, cm​2 and mm.

3. Concepts and skills

Which part of a loop gives the emf?

Only the edge cutting the field lines; the part outside the field gives none.

Why must the force be equal to B2l2v/R?

To keep the speed constant the applied force balances the magnetic force.

4. Mistakes to avoid

• Taking the length of the whole wire instead of the part in the field.

• Using the total emf when the loop is fully inside the field (it is zero).

• Forgetting unit conversions (cm, cm/s).

Does L depend on the current?

3. Concepts and skills

What is the emf between the ends of a rod turned about its middle?

Zero: the two halves give equal and opposite emfs.

When is the emf of a coil maximum?

When the plane of the coil is parallel to the field, that is, the flux is zero.

4. Mistakes to avoid

• Writing BL2ω and missing the factor 1/2.

• Using rev/min as ω without converting.

• Forgetting N in the coil.

3. Concepts and skills

How does an inductor behave at t=0 and at t=∞?

At t=0 it blocks the current (open circuit); at t=∞ it is a plain wire.

When is the rate of energy storage maximum?

At t=τln2, when I=E/2R.

4. Mistakes to avoid

• Using τ=RL or R/L.

• Putting the whole battery emf across the resistor at t=0.

• Forgetting the internal resistance of the battery.

2. Formulae and laws

Lenz's law

ε=−dΦdt, the induced current opposes the change

Slope rule

emf graph = slope of the Φ–t graph

3. Concepts and skills

When is there no induced emf?

When the flux does not change: uniform motion in a uniform field, or a constant current.

What happens to the sign when the magnet leaves?

The emf reverses, because the flux changes from increasing to decreasing.

4. Mistakes to avoid

• Thinking that motion alone gives an emf.

• Drawing the emf graph with the shape of the flux graph instead of its slope.
