3. Type-by-type detail

Type 1: Faraday's law: induced emf, current and heat from a changing flux – 21 questions

What is asked: A flux, a changing field or a changing area is given; find the induced emf, the current, the heat produced or the number of turns.

What to master: ε = −N dΦ/dt, Φ = BA cos θ, I = ε/R, the heat ∫ε²/R dt, and the flux through a loop of straight edges using the vector area.

Forms that appear: Φ = 5t² − 36t + 1 through 8 Ω at t = 2 s; Φ = 8t² − 9t + 5 through 20 Ω; a field changing in a square plate (graph) between 2 and 4 s; a coil with B going from 5000 T to 3000 T in 2 s; a circular loop in B = 3t³ĵ + 3t²k̂; the flux through the loop ABCDEFA in B = 3î + 4k̂.

Asked in: 2019 (1), 2020 (3), 2021 (2), 2022 (5), 2023 (6), 2024 (4)

Which form was asked:

Flux given as a function of time: emf, current and heat: 5 questions

Changing magnetic field through a fixed loop: Faraday's law: 13 questions

Changing area or orientation of the loop: 3 questions

Type 2: Motional emf in straight conductors, rods on rails and loops entering a field – 19 questions

What is asked: A wire, a bar or a loop moves in a magnetic field; find the emf, the current, the force, the power or the work done.

What to master: ε = B l v, I = ε/R, F = B²l²v/R, power = B²l²v²/R, and the moving edge as the only part cutting the field.

Forms that appear: a 5 m horizontal wire falling at 10 m/s in the earth's field; a metallic cube of side 15 cm at 2 m/s in 0.5 T; a conducting bar on rails with two resistors; a rod XY of 1 m on a circuit of 5 Ω in 0.15 T; a square loop of 15 cm entering a 50 cm wide field at 2 cm/s; a metallic ring moving into a field of 1 T.

Asked in: 2019 (4), 2020 (1), 2021 (4), 2023 (4), 2024 (4), 2025 (2)

Which form was asked:

emf of a straight conductor: e = B l v: 10 questions

Sliding rod on rails: current, force and power: 6 questions

Loop entering or leaving a field: 3 questions

Type 3: Self-inductance, emf across an inductor and energy stored – 17 questions

What is asked: Find the inductance, the average emf across a coil, or the energy stored from the data given for a coil or a circuit.

What to master: ε = L dI/dt, L = μ₀n²Al, U = ½LI², series combination L₁ + L₂ ± 2M, and the dependence of L on N, A and l.

Forms that appear: I = 3t + 8 giving 12 mV; a 12 V battery on a 6 Ω coil switched off in 1 ms giving 20 V; a 10 Ω, 20 mH coil carrying constant current opened in 100 μs; the energy for a current change from 10 A to 25 A; a triangular wooden frame scaled by 3; a coil with L = 2 H on V = 3t.

Asked in: 2019 (3), 2020 (2), 2021 (1), 2022 (3), 2023 (3), 2024 (2), 2025 (3)

Which form was asked:

Self-inductance from emf = L dI/dt: 8 questions

Inductance of a coil: dependence, combinations and statements: 5 questions

Energy stored in an inductor: 4 questions

Type 4: Rotating rods, discs and coils: emf of an AC generator – 13 questions

What is asked: A rod or a disc turns about an axis in a field, or a coil rotates; find the induced emf or its maximum value.

What to master: ε = ½BωL² for a rod or disc, ε_max = NBAω for a coil, ω = 2πf, and zero emf between the ends of a rod rotating about its middle in an axial field.

Forms that appear: a rod of length L rotated about one end in a normal field; a copper disc of radius 20 cm at 10π rad/s in 0.4 T; a 60 cm rod rotating about its perpendicular bisector; a coil of 100 turns, 14×10⁻² m², at 360 rev/min in 3.0 T; a circular coil of 1000 turns rotated once per second in 0.07 T; an elliptical loop rotated about the x-axis.

Asked in: 2020 (3), 2021 (1), 2022 (2), 2023 (4), 2024 (2), 2025 (1)

Which form was asked:

Rotating rod or disc: ε = ½ B ω L²: 5 questions

Rotating coil: ε = NBAω sin ωt: 8 questions

Type 5: LR circuits: growth, decay, energy and charge – 11 questions

What is asked: A battery, an inductor and resistors are switched; find the current at a time, the time constant, the energy stored, the rate of storing energy or the charge that flows.

What to master: I = (E/R)(1 − e^{−t/τ}), I = I₀e^{−t/τ}, τ = L/R, the inductor as an open circuit at t = 0 and a wire at t = ∞, and q = ∫I dt.

Forms that appear: two-way switch in a circuit with R = 6 Ω; a 10 mH, 0.1 Ω coil with a 0.9 Ω battery reaching 80% of the current; three resistors of 12 Ω and two inductors of 5 mH; the ratio of the currents at t = ∞ and t = 40 s; the charge through the battery between t = 0 and t = L/R.

Asked in: 2019 (4), 2020 (2), 2021 (3), 2023 (2)

Type 6: Mutual inductance of coils and loops – 9 questions

What is asked: Two coils or loops are placed together; find the mutual inductance, or the emf induced in one by a changing current in the other.

What to master: M = Φ₂/I₁, ε₂ = M dI₁/dt, the field at the centre of a circular or square loop, and the coaxial solenoids with M = μ₀n₁n₂πr₁²l.

Forms that appear: two concentric loops in the same plane; two coaxial solenoids of the same length; coils of mutual inductance 0.002 H with i = 5 sin 50πt; coils of 10 and 200 turns with radii 1 cm and 1000 cm; a small square loop inside a large square loop; a small circular loop inside a large square loop.

Asked in: 2019 (1), 2020 (1), 2021 (1), 2022 (1), 2023 (2), 2024 (3)

Type 7: Lenz's law, direction of the induced current and graphs of the induced emf – 8 questions

What is asked: A statement or a figure is given and you decide whether an emf is induced, in which direction the current flows, or which graph shows the emf.

What to master: The induced current opposes the change of flux, emf needs a changing flux, and the emf graph is the slope of the flux graph.

Forms that appear: which of four ways produces an induced emf in a coil; the flux of a current loop through the plane outside the coil and through the coil; a loop leaving a field and the plot of the emf; a bar magnet passing through a loop; the sources of a time varying magnetic field.

Asked in: 2020 (1), 2021 (3), 2023 (2), 2025 (2)

Type 8: Links to other chapters: electromagnetic waves and AC circuits – 4 questions

What is asked: Questions that use induction ideas in another chapter: the energy densities of a plane wave, or a coil on a DC and an AC supply.

What to master: The equal average electric and magnetic energy densities of a wave, the energy oscillating at twice the wave frequency, R from the DC test, and X_L = 2πfL with the 45° phase angle.

Forms that appear: the ratio of the average electric to the average magnetic energy density; how the energy of a wave in a small volume oscillates; a coil drawing 5 A on 20 V DC and 4 A on 20 V, 50 Hz AC; a coil with a reactance of 100 Ω at 1000 Hz and a 45° phase angle.

Asked in: 2020 (1), 2023 (2), 2024 (1)

5. Suggested study order for students

Start with the two largest families: Faraday's law with a changing flux (Type 1, 21 questions, 21%) and motional emf (Type 2, 19, 19%).

Then self-inductance and the energy stored (Type 3, 17) and rotating rods, discs and coils (Type 4, 13).

Then LR circuits (Type 5, 11) and mutual inductance (Type 6, 9).

Finish with Lenz's law and graphs (Type 7, 8) and the links to EM waves and AC (Type 8, 4).

6. A routine to follow for every question

Decide first which family the question belongs to: a changing flux, a moving straight conductor, a rotating rod or coil, an inductance, or an LR circuit.

Write the flux first, Φ = B·A cos θ, and differentiate it: ε = −dΦ/dt. Ask what is changing: B, the area or the angle.

For a moving conductor take B, l and v at right angles; for a rotating rod use ½BωL²; for a rotating coil NBAω.

Convert the units first (cm, mm, mH, μs, rev/min) and note which constants the question gives (π = 22/7, π = 3).

In an LR circuit the inductor opposes a change of current: no jump at t = 0, then I = (E/R)(1 − e^{−t/τ}) with τ = L/R.
