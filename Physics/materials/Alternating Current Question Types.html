How to read this: a “type” is the formula, law or technique that decides the answer. So every type below tells you what the question looks like and which formula or method to use.

3. Type-by-type detail

Type 1: Series LCR resonance: resonant frequency, current, power, bandwidth and quality factor – 30 questions

What is asked: Find the resonant frequency, the inductance or capacitance needed for resonance, the current and power at resonance, or the bandwidth and quality factor of the circuit.

What to master: ω₀ = 1/√(LC), Z = R at resonance, I = V/R, P = V²/R, Q = ω₀L/R, bandwidth R/L, and the effect of changing R, L or C.

Forms that appear: an inductor of 0.5 mH and a capacitor of 200 μF: the resonant frequency; the power dissipated at resonance for 250 V; the capacitance that maximises the average power; a coil of 40 mH and a capacitor of 100 μF; the current amplitude when R is halved; the quality factor of a circuit with R = 100 Ω, L = 1 H.

Asked in: 2020 (1), 2021 (12), 2022 (5), 2023 (7), 2024 (3), 2025 (2)

Which form was asked:

Resonant frequency and the L or C needed for resonance: 13 questions

Current, power and voltage at resonance; effect of changing R: 10 questions

Quality factor, bandwidth and sharpness of resonance: 7 questions

Type 2: Impedance, current, phase and voltages in series and parallel AC circuits – 24 questions

What is asked: Find the reactances, the impedance, the current, the voltage across an element or the phase angle in a series LCR circuit, or the current in a circuit with parallel branches.

What to master: X_L = ωL, X_C = 1/(ωC), Z = √(R² + (X_L − X_C)²), I = V/Z, tan φ = (X_L − X_C)/R, and the rules for very high frequency.

Forms that appear: L = 100 mH, C = 100 μF and R = 10 Ω on a 220 V, 50 Hz source: the current; a series circuit with X_L = 200 Ω, X_C = 100 Ω, R = 100 Ω; the rms voltage across the capacitor; a lamp in series with a capacitor; the phase between two branches; the effective impedance at very high frequency.

Asked in: 2019 (2), 2020 (1), 2021 (7), 2022 (7), 2023 (2), 2024 (3), 2025 (2)

Which form was asked:

Impedance, current and voltage across elements in a series circuit: 16 questions

Phase angle and phase difference between current and voltage: 4 questions

Parallel branches and behaviour at very high frequency: 4 questions

Type 3: Statements, matching, graphs and phasors – 17 questions

What is asked: Judge statements and assertion-reason items on AC circuits, match the lists of elements, phases and devices, or read graphs, figures and phasor diagrams.

What to master: The behaviour of R, L and C in AC, the phase relations, the condition and effects of resonance, and the shape of the X_L, X_C and impedance graphs.

Forms that appear: two statements on resonance and power; match current and voltage phases with the elements; the phasor diagram for a capacitor; the graph of impedance against frequency; the I–ω curve of a series LCR circuit.

Asked in: 2021 (4), 2022 (3), 2023 (6), 2024 (3), 2025 (1)

Which form was asked:

Statements and assertion-reason: 7 questions

Match the list: 6 questions

Graphs, figures and phasors: 4 questions

Type 4: RMS, peak and instantaneous values of alternating current – 15 questions

What is asked: Find the rms value of a current given as a sum of terms, the time to change from one value to another, or compare the heat produced by direct and alternating currents.

What to master: I_rms = I₀/√2, the rms of i = a + b sin ωt and of a sin ωt + b cos ωt, and the phase ωt at each instant.

Forms that appear: i = 6 + √56 sin(100πt + π/3); a sin ωt + b cos ωt; the time for the current to go from its maximum to its rms value on a 220 V, 50 Hz source; the time to rise from half the peak to the peak; a hot-wire ammeter.

Asked in: 2019 (1), 2021 (5), 2022 (3), 2024 (2), 2025 (4)

Which form was asked:

RMS value of composite currents and the ammeter reading: 6 questions

Time between two values of an alternating quantity: 6 questions

Peak, rms and heating comparison: 3 questions

Type 5: Power, power factor and wattless current – 14 questions

What is asked: Find the average power, the power factor or the energy dissipated in an AC circuit, or identify a circuit that carries wattless current.

What to master: P = V_rms I_rms cos φ = I_rms² R, cos φ = R/Z, and the effect of the frequency on X_L and X_C.

Forms that appear: V = 100 sin 100t and I = 100 sin(100t + π/3) mA: the average power; an LR circuit with power factor 1/√2 when the frequency doubles; R = 80 Ω, X_L = 70 Ω, X_C = 130 Ω: the power factor; a coil with a DC resistance and an AC reactance.

Asked in: 2020 (1), 2021 (2), 2022 (2), 2023 (2), 2024 (6), 2025 (1)

Which form was asked:

Average power, wattless current and power dissipated: 7 questions

Power factor of LR and LCR circuits: 7 questions

Type 6: Pure inductor, pure capacitor and RL or RC circuits: reactance, phase and displacement current – 13 questions

What is asked: Find the reactance, the current or the voltage equation for a pure inductor or capacitor, the displacement current in a capacitor, or the phase of an RL or RC circuit.

What to master: X_L = ωL, X_C = 1/(ωC), the current lags by π/2 in an inductor and leads in a capacitor, tan φ = ωL/R or 1/(ωRC).

Forms that appear: V = 260 sin 628t across 5 mH: X_L; a 150 μF capacitor on 36 sin 120πt: the maximum current; the conduction and displacement current of a 200 pF capacitor; i = 5 sin(49πt − 30°) in 30 mH: the voltage equation.

Asked in: 2019 (1), 2020 (1), 2021 (2), 2022 (5), 2023 (2), 2024 (2)

Which form was asked:

Pure inductor: reactance and current: 5 questions

Pure capacitor and displacement current: 5 questions

RL and RC circuits and phase conditions: 3 questions

Type 7: Growth and decay of current, LC oscillations and RC output – 12 questions

What is asked: Find the current in an LR circuit just after closing, long after, or at a given time, the time for the energy or current to reach a fraction, the maximum current of an LC circuit, or the output of an RC circuit with a diode or square wave.

What to master: i = i₀(1 − e^{−t/τ}), i = i₀e^{−t/τ}, τ = L/R, I_max = Q ω₀, and the inductor as an open circuit at t = 0 and a wire at t = ∞.

Forms that appear: the current through the battery just after closing the switch; the steady-state current with inductors in parallel; the time for the magnetic energy to reach 25 % of its maximum; a 500 μF capacitor charged to 100 V connected to a 50 mH inductor; two capacitors discharging through a diode.

Asked in: 2019 (1), 2020 (2), 2021 (5), 2022 (3), 2023 (1)

Which form was asked:

Growth and decay of current in an LR circuit: 8 questions

LC oscillations and the output of an RC circuit: 4 questions

Type 8: Transformers – 10 questions

What is asked: Find the secondary voltage or current, the number of turns, the load resistance, or the output current of a transformer with a given efficiency.

What to master: V_s/V_p = N_s/N_p = I_p/I_s, efficiency = P_out/P_in, and the load V²/P.

Forms that appear: a transformer with 300 and 150 turns giving 2.2 kW: the input voltage and current; an ideal transformer at 12 kV supplying 60 kW at 120 V: the load; a step-down transformer of 90 % efficiency with 5 A in the primary.

6. Suggested study order for students

Start with the two largest families: resonance (Type 1, 30 questions, 22%) and impedance, current and phase (Type 2, 24, 17%).

Then the statements, matching and graphs (Type 3, 17 questions) and the rms and instantaneous values (Type 4, 15).

Then power and power factor (Type 5, 14) and the single elements (Type 6, 13).

Finish with growth and decay of current (Type 7, 12), transformers (Type 8, 10) and the one-off questions.

7. A routine to follow for every question

Decide first which family the question belongs to: rms and instantaneous values, a single element, a series LCR circuit, resonance, power factor, a transient, or a transformer.

Write X_L = ωL and X_C = 1/(ωC) with ω = 2πf before anything else, and watch the units (μF, nF, mH).

Decide whether the voltage given is the rms or the peak value; use the rms value for rms current and power.

At resonance X_L = X_C, Z = R and the current is the largest; the power is V²/R.

For a switch closed in an LR circuit use: just after, the inductor is open; long after, it is a wire.
