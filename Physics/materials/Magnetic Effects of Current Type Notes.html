2. Formulae and laws

Radius of the circle

r=mvqB=pqB=2mKqB

After acceleration through V

r=1B2mVq

Period and frequency

T=2πmqB, f=qB2πm (independent of v and r)

Magnetic force

F⃗=q(v⃗×B⃗), F=qvBsinθ

Same K, same V, same p, same v

r∝mq, m/q, 1q, mq

3. Concepts and skills

Does the magnetic force change the speed?

No. It is perpendicular to the velocity, does no work, and changes only the direction of motion.

How do I compare two particles?

Write r in terms of the quantity that is the same for both (the kinetic energy, the potential or the momentum) and cancel it.

What happens if the velocity is along B⃗?

The force is zero and the particle goes straight.

Which sign for the electron?

Put q=−e in q(v⃗×B⃗); the force on the electron is opposite to that on a proton.

4. Mistakes to avoid

• Using r∝m/q when the kinetic energy (not the speed) is the same.

• Forgetting that the alpha particle has charge 2e and mass 4mp.

• Forgetting the minus sign of the electron's charge in the cross product.

• Taking the period to depend on the speed.

3. Concepts and skills

What does a straight lead contribute at the centre of an arc?

Nothing if the lead lies along a radius (the angle between dl⃗ and r⃗ is 0).

How do I add the fields of two arcs?

Find the direction of each by the right-hand rule: the same sense add, opposite senses subtract.

What changes when a wire is rewound into more turns?

The radius becomes 1/N of the original, so B=μ0NI/2R∝N2.

4. Mistakes to avoid

• Using μ0I/2R for an arc instead of μ0Iθ/4πR.

• Adding the fields of two arcs without checking their directions.

• Forgetting the number of turns N.

• Putting the radius equal to the length of the arc.

3. Concepts and skills

Which current goes into Ampere's law?

Only the current enclosed by the Amperian loop.

Where is the field of a wire largest?

At the surface; it rises linearly inside and falls as 1/r outside.

What is the field of a hollow cylinder inside?

Zero: no current is enclosed.

4. Mistakes to avoid

• Using 1/r for the field inside a solid wire.

• Using the total current instead of the enclosed current.

• Using N instead of n=N/L in μ0nI.

• Taking the field outside a coaxial cable as non-zero for equal and opposite currents.

3. Concepts and skills

Do parallel wires attract or repel?

Currents in the same direction attract; in opposite directions repel.

What is the force on a bent wire?

The same as on the straight wire joining its two ends.

What is the net force on a closed loop in a uniform field?

Zero, though the torque need not be zero.

4. Mistakes to avoid

• Using the length of the arc instead of the chord for a bent wire.

• Forgetting sinθ when the wire is not perpendicular to the field.

• Mixing up the factor 2π in the force between two wires.

• Taking the force on a loop in a uniform field as non-zero.

3. Concepts and skills

Why does the velocity selector not depend on the charge?

Both forces are proportional to q, so q cancels.

Which component makes the pitch?

The component along B⃗, which is not changed by the field.

Is the cyclotron frequency constant?

Yes; it does not depend on the speed or the radius.

4. Mistakes to avoid

• Using the full speed for the radius of a helix instead of vsinθ.

• Using vsinθ for the pitch instead of vcosθ.

• Forgetting that the forces of the two fields must be opposite for undeflected motion.

• Using the radius of the dee as the radius of every orbit.

• Using the period formula with T∝M instead of T∝1/M.

What is the apothem?

The perpendicular distance from the centre to a side; it is the distance in the segment formula.

How do the fields of two wires combine at the midpoint?

Same direction currents subtract; opposite direction currents add.

What is the field at the end of a semi-infinite wire?

μ0I/(4πd).

4. Mistakes to avoid

• Using the side instead of the apothem as d.

• Forgetting to multiply by the number of sides.

• Subtracting the fields when the currents are opposite.

• Using the infinite-wire formula for a short segment.

3. Concepts and skills

When is the torque largest?

When the plane of the coil is parallel to the field.

Why does doubling N not double the voltage sensitivity?

The resistance doubles as well, so NAB/kR is unchanged.

How do I raise the current sensitivity?

More turns, a stronger field, a larger area, a weaker spring.

4. Mistakes to avoid

• Using θ between the plane (not the normal) and the field.

• Forgetting the number of turns.

• Thinking that more turns always raise the voltage sensitivity.

• Using a shunt of large resistance to make an ammeter.
