JEE Main Physics – Semiconductors

Types of questions asked in JEE Main 2019–2025 (168 questions)

Key message: these 168 questions fall into only 8 repeating types. The three biggest types (Types 1, 2, 3) account for 84 questions (50%). The Zener and diode types (1, 2) are 59 questions (35%); the three logic-gate types (4, 5, 6) are 56 (33%). About 20% of the questions ask for a numerical (integer) answer, so there are no options to guess from.

How to read this: a “type” is the formula, law or technique that decides the answer. So every type below tells you what the question looks like and which formula or method to use.

3. Type-by-type detail

Type 1: Zener diode regulator circuits – 30 questions

What is asked: A Zener diode is connected with a series resistance and a load; find the current, the voltage, the power dissipated or the resistance needed.

What to master: The Zener voltage stays fixed in breakdown, so the series-resistor current is (V_in − V_Z)/R, the load current is V_Z/R_L and the Zener current is the difference; the power rating fixes the maximum Zener current.

Forms that appear: a 5 V Zener on a 25 V supply with 400 Ω in series, the minimum series resistance for a 25 mA maximum Zener current, a 1.6 W or 2 W Zener, the power in a Zener, two Zeners in series, a Zener in the circuit of a potential divider with an LED.

Asked in: 2019 (5), 2020 (4), 2021 (8), 2022 (4), 2023 (2), 2024 (5), 2025 (2)

Which form was asked:

Current and voltage in a Zener regulator circuit: 14 questions

Power rating, maximum Zener current and the series resistance: 10 questions

Zener breakdown, two Zeners and Zener with other components: statements and circuits: 6 questions

Type 2: Diode circuits: biasing, resistance and I-V curves – 29 questions

What is asked: Decide whether a diode is forward or reverse biased, find the current, voltage or equivalent resistance in a circuit with ideal or real diodes, or read an I-V curve or an output waveform.

What to master: Ideal diode as a wire or an open circuit, cut-in voltage of Ge and Si, forward resistance, dynamic resistance from the slope of the I-V curve, and rectification or clipping of an AC input.

Forms that appear: which circuit is forward biased, two diodes with forward resistance 25 Ω or 50 Ω in a network, a Ge and a Si diode in series, the dynamic resistance at 3 mA, the waveform across a resistor with an ideal diode, a capacitor across a full-wave rectifier.

Asked in: 2019 (2), 2020 (4), 2021 (9), 2022 (5), 2023 (1), 2024 (4), 2025 (4)

Which form was asked:

Forward or reverse bias: choosing the biased circuit or the device: 4 questions

Current, voltage and equivalent resistance with ideal and real diodes: 17 questions

Cut-in voltage, dynamic resistance, I-V curve and output waveforms: 6 questions

Statements on rectifier filtering and the junction diode: 2 questions

Type 3: Transistor characteristics, gains and switching [OOS] – 25 questions

What is asked: A transistor in the common-emitter mode: find α, β, the current, voltage or power gain, the base current or the output voltage, or read an input, transfer or output characteristic. These questions carry the book's out-of-syllabus mark.

What to master: β = ΔI_C/ΔI_B, α = β/(1 + β), A_V = βR_out/R_in, power gain = βA_V (or 10 log₁₀), the saturation condition I_B = V_CC/(βR_C), and the active, cut-off and saturation regions.

Forms that appear: collector current from 5 mA to 16 mA for a base current change of 100 μA, 60 dB power gain, a 10 mV signal giving a 3 mA change in the collector current, the minimum base current for saturation with β = 250, β from the output characteristic at V_CE = 10 V.

Asked in: 2019 (5), 2020 (1), 2021 (7), 2022 (8), 2023 (4)

Which form was asked:

Current gains α and β and the change of collector current: 8 questions

Voltage gain and power gain of a CE amplifier: 8 questions

Base current, saturation and output voltage of a CE circuit: 5 questions

Regions of operation and statements on the transistor: 4 questions

Type 4: Logic outputs for given inputs and waveforms – 23 questions

What is asked: A circuit of gates is given with the inputs; find the output, the inputs that make the output 0 or 1, or draw the output waveform for given input waveforms.

What to master: Step-by-step output through each gate (NOT, AND, OR, NAND, NOR, XOR), the four input combinations, and the interval-by-interval reading of waveforms.

Forms that appear: the output Y = 0 only when …, the output of a combination of NAND and NOR gates for A = 1 and B = 0, the output of Z for inputs (A, B) = (0, 1), the input values that give output 1 at R, the output waveform of a NAND gate or of a gate combination.

Asked in: 2019 (2), 2020 (2), 2021 (4), 2022 (4), 2023 (4), 2024 (4), 2025 (3)

Which form was asked:

Output of a gate circuit for given inputs, and the inputs that give a stated output: 17 questions

Output waveform for given input waveforms: 6 questions

Type 5: Identifying and converting logic gates – 18 questions

What is asked: A gate circuit is given; name the gate it performs, find the equivalent gate or circuit, or write its Boolean expression.

What to master: The gate identities (NAND and NOR as universal gates, De Morgan's laws), and the Boolean expression of a combination.

Forms that appear: a NAND followed by a NOT, two NOR gates with a common input, a NAND gate with both inputs joined, the gate equivalent to the circuit shown, the circuit that realises Y = AB'C + A'C'.

Asked in: 2019 (1), 2020 (2), 2021 (5), 2022 (1), 2023 (4), 2024 (2), 2025 (3)

Which form was asked:

Name the gate performed by a circuit: 8 questions

Equivalent gate or equivalent circuit: 8 questions

Boolean expression of a circuit: 2 questions

Type 6: Truth tables of gate combinations – 15 questions

What is asked: Choose the correct truth table for a given gate circuit, or the circuit that gives a given truth table.

What to master: Writing the four rows A B = 00, 01, 10, 11 and the output of every gate in each row; recognising the standard columns of AND, OR, NAND, NOR and XOR.

Forms that appear: the truth table of four NOR gates, of a NAND combination with an inverter, the gate for a truth table with output 0 1 1 1, the output sequence for four input pairs.

Asked in: 2019 (1), 2021 (4), 2023 (2), 2024 (4), 2025 (4)

Which form was asked:

Truth table of a gate combination: 13 questions

Circuit that gives a given truth table: 2 questions

Type 7: Semiconductor and p-n junction basics – 14 questions

What is asked: Questions on intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors, doping and carriers, the effect of temperature, or the p-n junction (barrier, depletion layer, diffusion and drift, light on the junction).

What to master: Majority and minority carriers, n_e n_h = n_i², the position of the Fermi level, the fall of resistance with temperature, the electric field in the depletion layer, and the photon energy needed to create a hole.

Forms that appear: the electron density in a doped semiconductor with n_i = 1.5×10¹⁶ m⁻³, match the Fermi level with the semiconductor type, assertion–reason on doping, the electric field across a 0.6 V barrier 6 μm wide, a multimeter to identify a diode.

Asked in: 2021 (4), 2022 (3), 2023 (5), 2024 (1), 2025 (1)

Which form was asked:

Intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors, doping, carriers and temperature: 8 questions

The p-n junction: barrier, depletion layer, diffusion and drift, photon energy: 6 questions

Type 8: Photodiode, LED and solar cell – 12 questions

What is asked: Find the wavelength emitted by an LED or the band gap from the cut-off wavelength of a photodiode, or choose the correct statement on the photodiode, LED and solar cell.

What to master: E = hc/λ, the colour of an LED, the biasing (photodiode reverse, LED forward, solar cell none), and the I-V characteristic of the solar cell.

Forms that appear: the wavelength of the light from a GaAs LED with a band gap of 1.42 eV, the band gap for violet light, a photodiode that responds below 660 nm, which statements on solar cell, photodiode and LED are correct.

Asked in: 2020 (2), 2021 (1), 2022 (3), 2023 (3), 2024 (2), 2025 (1)

Which form was asked:

Band gap and the wavelength of light from an LED or for a photodiode: 5 questions

Statements on the photodiode, LED and solar cell: 7 questions

5. The few genuinely new questions

These do not fit a repeating pattern. See each once; they do not need heavy practice.

Which of the given logic operations is reversible: a gate whose output lets you recover the inputs (the NOT gate) (2020)

The feedback needed by an amplifier to work as an oscillator: positive feedback, where part of the output is returned in phase with the input [OOS] (2022)

6. Suggested study order for students

Start with the diode circuits (Type 2) and the Zener circuits (Type 1): 59 questions (35%). They are all Ohm's law once you know which diodes conduct.

Then the logic gates (Types 4, 5 and 6): 56 questions (33%). Learn the five truth tables and De Morgan's laws, and practise tracing a circuit row by row.

Then the transistor (Type 3): 25 questions, all marked out of syllabus in the book; do these last.

Finish with the semiconductor and p-n junction basics (Type 7) and the photodiode, LED and solar cell (Type 8): 26 questions, and the two one-off questions.

7. A routine to follow for every question

Decide which family the question belongs to: a Zener or diode circuit, a transistor, a logic gate circuit, or a statement on a semiconductor device.

For a diode or Zener circuit decide first whether each diode is on (forward biased, or the Zener in breakdown) and redraw the circuit with a wire or an open circuit in its place.

For a transistor use I_C = βI_B first, then V_CE = V_CC − I_C R_C; check the saturation limit.

For gates write the output of each gate on the figure, row by row for 00, 01, 10 and 11.

For band gap and wavelength questions convert with E (eV) = 1240/λ (nm) and check which way the inequality goes.
