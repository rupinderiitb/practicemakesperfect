Types of questions asked in JEE Main 2019–2025 (285 questions)

Key message:

  • These 285 questions fall into only 8 repeating types (plus a small group of conceptual or one-off questions).
  • The three biggest types (Types 1, 2, 3) account for 143 questions (50%).
  • Networks, cells/Kirchhoff and wire resistance (Types 1, 4, 3) are 140 questions (49%)

  • 101 questions (35%) are numerical (integer) answer; so, there are no options to guess from
  • About 90% of the questions are decided by applying a formula, 7% by concept or reasoning, and the rest need both.

(How to read this: in Physics a “type” is the law or formula that unlocks the question. For each ‘type’ the document says what the question looks like, which laws and formulas you need, and how often it was asked)

3. Type-by-type detail

Type 1: Equivalent resistance and currents in resistor networks – 53 questions

What is asked: Find the equivalent resistance of a network (or a wire frame), or the current and voltage in a part of it once a battery is connected.

What to master: Redraw the circuit to spot series/parallel groups, use symmetry (equal potentials) for frames and cubes, and use current division to go back from the total current to a branch.

Formulas and laws: R_s = ΣR; 1/R_p = Σ1/R; I₁ = I·R₂/(R₁+R₂); V = IR

Forms that appear: balanced-looking networks, wire bent into a triangle or polygon, twelve resistors forming a cube, choosing a combination of three resistors for a given value

Style: 21 of 53 ask for an integer answer; 52 are formula-based and 1 concept-based.

Asked in: 2019 (5), 2020 (2), 2021 (10), 2022 (8), 2023 (13), 2024 (9), 2025 (6)

Which form was asked:

Equivalent resistance by series–parallel reduction; choosing a combination: 22 questions

Symmetric frames and wires bent into shapes (triangle, square, polygon, cube): 8 questions

Currents and potential differences in a resistor network with a battery: 23 questions

Type 2: Galvanometer, ammeter and voltmeter – 46 questions

What is asked: Convert a galvanometer into an ammeter or voltmeter, find the reading of a real meter in a circuit, or handle sensitivity and deflection.

What to master: Shunt in parallel for ammeter, resistor in series for voltmeter. Include the meter's own resistance when finding readings. Know the definitions of current and voltage sensitivity.

Formulas and laws: S = I_gG/(I − I_g); R = V/I_g − G; current sensitivity = θ/I = NBA/C

Forms that appear: shunt for a 0–3 A range, series resistance for a 0–1 V range, voltmeter reading across a resistor in a divider, figure of merit and half-deflection method

Style: 9 of 46 ask for an integer answer; 40 are formula-based and 6 concept-based.

Asked in: 2019 (10), 2020 (7), 2021 (2), 2022 (4), 2023 (8), 2024 (13), 2025 (2)

Which form was asked:

Converting a galvanometer into an ammeter or voltmeter: 20 questions

Meters in a circuit: loading, readings and error: 12 questions

Galvanometer sensitivity, figure of merit and deflection: 14 questions

Type 3: Resistance of a wire: resistivity, stretching, temperature and colour code – 44 questions

What is asked: How the resistance of a wire changes when it is stretched, cut or heated, the resistance of a given shape from ρ, and reading the colour code.

What to master: R = ρl/A with constant volume when a wire is stretched; R_T = R₀(1 + αΔT); the colour-code table.

Formulas and laws: R = ρl/A; R ∝ l² (constant volume); R_T = R₀(1 + αΔT)

Forms that appear: wire stretched to double its length, wire cut into five parts joined in parallel, resistance at two temperatures, hollow cylinder, colour-coded resistor

Style: 13 of 44 ask for an integer answer; 31 are formula-based and 7 concept-based.

Asked in: 2019 (8), 2020 (1), 2021 (9), 2022 (8), 2023 (8), 2024 (7), 2025 (3)

Which form was asked:

Stretching, cutting, drawing and re-shaping a wire: 14 questions

Resistivity and the dimensions of a conductor: 12 questions

Temperature dependence, materials and R–T graphs: 12 questions

Colour code of resistors: 6 questions

Type 4: Cells and Kirchhoff's laws – 43 questions

What is asked: Terminal voltage and current when cells with internal resistance are joined in various ways, and potential differences or currents in multi-loop circuits.

What to master: V = E − Ir; rules for series, parallel and opposing cells; Kirchhoff's junction and loop rules; behaviour of capacitors in steady state.

Formulas and laws: V = E − Ir; ΣI = 0 at a junction; ΣΔV = 0 around a loop

Forms that appear: two cells in opposition, identical cells in series/parallel giving the same current, current in the middle branch of a two-loop circuit, potential at a point in a part of a circuit

Style: 19 of 43 ask for an integer answer; 42 are formula-based and 1 concept-based.

Asked in: 2019 (7), 2020 (5), 2021 (10), 2022 (7), 2023 (7), 2024 (3), 2025 (4)

Which form was asked:

Cells: emf, internal resistance, series/parallel/opposing combinations: 19 questions

Kirchhoff's laws and potential differences in circuits: 20 questions

Circuits with capacitors or time-varying sources: 4 questions

Type 5: Electric power and the heating effect of current – 34 questions

What is asked: Heat or power in a resistor when the current or voltage changes, brightness of bulbs in series or parallel, and power and efficiency with a cell.

What to master: P = VI = I²R = V²/R; H = I²Rt; the rated resistance of a bulb; maximum power transfer at R = r; efficiency of transmission.

Formulas and laws: P = V²/R = I²R; H = Pt; R_bulb = V²/P; P_max when R = r

Forms that appear: bulbs of 25 W and 100 W in series, appliance run at a lower voltage, heating wire cut in half, power line delivering 1 kW at 220 V

Style: 11 of 34 ask for an integer answer; 33 are formula-based and 1 concept-based.

Asked in: 2019 (4), 2020 (2), 2021 (7), 2022 (4), 2023 (5), 2024 (9), 2025 (3)

Which form was asked:

Heating effect: H = I²Rt = V²t/R: 14 questions

Bulbs and appliances (rated power and voltage): 12 questions

Power from cells, efficiency and transmission: 8 questions

Type 6: Meter bridge and Wheatstone bridge – 23 questions

What is asked: Find the unknown resistance from the balance length of a meter bridge, or use the balance condition of a Wheatstone bridge.

What to master: P/Q = R/S for a balanced bridge; for a meter bridge R/S = l/(100 − l); effect of interchanging resistors or adding a resistor in parallel on the null point.

Formulas and laws: P/Q = R/S; X = S(100 − l)/l

Forms that appear: null point at 40 cm, resistor shunted to shift the null point, bridge with colour-coded resistor, current through the galvanometer arm

Style: 10 of 23 ask for an integer answer; 23 are formula-based and 0 concept-based.

Asked in: 2019 (5), 2020 (5), 2021 (2), 2022 (5), 2023 (1), 2024 (5)

Which form was asked:

Meter bridge: 13 questions

Wheatstone bridge: balance condition and unbalanced networks: 10 questions

Type 7: Potentiometer – 21 questions

What is asked: Compare two emfs, find internal resistance, or find the balance length from the potential gradient of a potentiometer wire.

What to master: The potential gradient k = V/L; at the null point emf = k·l; effect of series or shunt resistances on k and on the balance length.

Formulas and laws: E₁/E₂ = l₁/l₂; r = R(l₁ − l₂)/l₂; k = IR_wire/L

Forms that appear: emf of two cells from balance lengths, internal resistance from shunting the cell, wire connected with a resistance box

Style: 10 of 21 ask for an integer answer; 20 are formula-based and 1 concept-based.

Asked in: 2019 (3), 2020 (3), 2021 (4), 2022 (6), 2023 (3), 2024 (1), 2025 (1)

Which form was asked:

Potentiometer: comparing emfs and finding internal resistance: 9 questions

Potentiometer circuit: potential gradient, current and balance length: 12 questions

Type 8: Electric current and drift of electrons – 19 questions

What is asked: Charge that flows when the current changes with time, and the relation between current, drift velocity, electron density, mobility and current density.

What to master: Q = ∫I dt, I = nAev_d, J = σE, mobility = v_d/E; reasoning on how v_d changes with temperature or area.

Formulas and laws: I = nAev_d; J = I/A; μ = v_d/E; Q = ∫I dt

Forms that appear: I = 3t² + 4t³ over a time interval, copper wire with n = 8×10²⁸, drift speed from the current, current in the outer part of a wire with a given J

Style: 6 of 19 ask for an integer answer; 16 are formula-based and 3 concept-based.

Asked in: 2019 (4), 2021 (4), 2022 (3), 2023 (5), 2024 (2), 2025 (1)

Which form was asked:

Charge passed from a time-varying current: 5 questions

Drift velocity, electron density, mobility and current density: 14 questions

6. Suggested study order for students

Start with resistor networks, cells and Kirchhoff’s laws, and wire resistance (Types 1, 4, 3): 140 questions (49%). Everything else uses these.

Then galvanometer, ammeter and voltmeter, the potentiometer and the bridges (Types 2, 7, 6): 90 questions (32%).

Then power and heating (Type 5), which only needs P = V²/R and P = I²R.

Finish with current and drift velocity (Type 8): few questions and short formulas.

7. A routine to follow for every question

Redraw the circuit so that every series and parallel group is clear; mark the current direction in each branch.

Reduce the network to one equivalent resistance, using symmetry and balanced bridges to simplify.

Find the total current (use E − Ir for cells), then go back using current division and V = IR.

For meters, include the meter resistance. For power questions pick P = I²R or V²/R according to what stays constant.

Check the answer with units and with a limit (r → 0, R very large).
