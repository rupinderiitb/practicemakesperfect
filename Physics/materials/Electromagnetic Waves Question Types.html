JEE Main Physics – Electromagnetic Waves

Types of questions asked in JEE Main 2019–2025 (114 questions)

Key message: these 114 questions fall into only 7 repeating types. The three biggest types (Types 1, 2, 3) account for 69 questions (61%). The questions on the fields of the wave (Types 1 and 5) are 42 (37%); intensity and energy (Type 2) is 20 (18%). About 11% of the questions ask for a numerical (integer) answer, so there are no options to guess from.

How to read this: a “type” is the formula, law or technique that decides the answer. So every type below tells you what the question looks like and which formula or method to use.

3. Type-by-type detail

Type 1: Directions of E, B and propagation, and the field equation of the other field – 32 questions

What is asked: A plane wave is given by one of its fields (an equation, or the values at a point); find the direction of travel, the direction or value of the other field, or write its equation.

What to master: E × B along the direction of travel, E₀ = cB₀, the same phase for both fields, and the sign of the phase (kx − ωt for +x, kx + ωt for −x).

Forms that appear: E along +y and B along +z, E along −z and B along +x; E = 9.6 ĵ V/m at 35 MHz along x: B = ?; B = 3×10⁻⁸ sin(1.6×10³x + 48×10¹⁰t) ĵ T: E = ?; E = 57 cos[…](4î − 3ĵ): B = ?; the wave with polarisation k̂ travelling along (î + ĵ)/√2.

Asked in: 2019 (5), 2020 (8), 2021 (4), 2022 (3), 2023 (4), 2024 (5), 2025 (3)

Which form was asked:

Directions of E, B and propagation (right-hand rule): 9 questions

Value of the other field at a point, with its direction: 5 questions

Writing the equation of the other field: 18 questions

Type 2: Intensity, energy density and power of a wave – 20 questions

What is asked: Find the intensity, the energy density or the energy in a volume of a wave from its field amplitude, or from the power of a source at a distance.

What to master: I = ½ε₀cE₀², the electric and magnetic energy densities are equal, total average density ½ε₀E₀², point source I = P/(4πr²).

Forms that appear: intensity of E = 600 sin(ωt − kx); a 27 mW laser of 10 mm² cross-section: E₀; a 100 W source with 5% efficiency at 5 m; energy in a cylinder; the unit of √(2I/ε₀c).

Asked in: 2019 (3), 2020 (2), 2021 (4), 2022 (3), 2023 (3), 2024 (3), 2025 (2)

Which form was asked:

Intensity from the field amplitude or from the power and area of a beam: 4 questions

Energy density and energy in a volume: 8 questions

Point source: intensity at a distance and the field amplitudes: 3 questions

Field amplitude from the intensity, and units: 5 questions

Type 3: The electromagnetic spectrum: order, uses, sources and X-rays – 17 questions

What is asked: Arrange the bands by wavelength, match bands with their uses, sources or wavelength ranges, or answer a question on X-rays and photons.

What to master: The order of the spectrum and the wavelength range of each band, the source and the use of each, λ_min = 12400/V (Å) and E = hc/λ.

Forms that appear: match microwave, UV, X-ray and infrared with their wavelength ranges; arrange gamma, X-rays, infrared and microwaves in ascending wavelength; the source of X-rays; the shortest wavelength of an X-ray tube at 1.24 million volts.

Asked in: 2019 (1), 2020 (3), 2021 (4), 2022 (3), 2023 (4), 2024 (2)

Which form was asked:

Order of the bands, uses and sources: matching and arranging: 12 questions

Colour, X-rays and photon questions: 5 questions

Type 4: Maxwell's equations, displacement current and statements on EM waves – 15 questions

What is asked: Identify the Maxwell equation or the law from its integral form, find a displacement current, or judge statements on the properties of electromagnetic waves.

What to master: The four equations in integral form and what each says, i_d = ε₀dΦ_E/dt and its equality with the conduction current, and the properties of EM waves.

Forms that appear: match Gauss's law, Faraday's law, Ampere–Maxwell law with their equations; the equation valid for time-varying but not static fields; the displacement current through part of a capacitor; statements on E, B and the direction of propagation.

Asked in: 2021 (1), 2022 (2), 2023 (6), 2024 (4), 2025 (2)

Which form was asked:

Maxwell's equations and displacement current: 6 questions

Statements and assertion–reason on EM waves: 9 questions

Type 5: Amplitudes of the electric and magnetic fields and wave parameters – 10 questions

What is asked: Find the amplitude of E from B (or B from E) of a wave, or its wavelength, frequency or speed, using the ratio of the amplitudes.

What to master: E₀ = cB₀ (or vB₀ for a wave of speed v) and v = ω/k = fλ.

Forms that appear: B₀ = 6×10⁻⁷ T: E₀ = ?; E = 6 V/m at 30 MHz: B = x×10⁻⁸ T; a 60 MHz wave in air: λ = ?; E₀ = 540 V/m: B₀ = ?; B = 100×10⁻⁶ sin[…]: E₀ = ?.

Asked in: 2019 (3), 2021 (1), 2022 (2), 2023 (2), 2024 (1), 2025 (1)

Type 6: Radiation pressure and force on a charge in the wave – 10 questions

What is asked: Light of known intensity falls on a surface: find the pressure or force; or a charge moves in the wave: find the force on it.

What to master: Pressure I/c (absorbed) or 2I/c (reflected), F = q(E + v × B), and the ratio c/v of the electric to the magnetic force.

Forms that appear: sunlight on 36 cm² exerting 7.2×10⁻⁹ N; 25% reflected and 75% absorbed; the ratio of electric to magnetic force on a charge at 3×10⁷ m/s; the force on a particle at 0.8c in two waves.

Asked in: 2019 (2), 2020 (1), 2021 (4), 2022 (2), 2024 (1)

Which form was asked:

Radiation pressure and force exerted by light on a surface: 5 questions

Force on a charged particle in the wave: 5 questions

Type 7: Speed and wavelength in a medium: permittivity, permeability and refractive index – 8 questions

What is asked: A wave travels in a medium of given permittivity and permeability, or at a given speed: find the speed, the permittivity, the refractive index, the wavelength or the ratio of fields.

What to master: v = 1/√(με), n = √(μ_rε_r), constant frequency, E₀/H₀ = √(μ/ε) and the change of the field amplitudes at an interface.

Forms that appear: speed 2×10⁸ m/s with μ_r = 1: ε_r = ?; μ_r = 10/π, ε_r = 1/0.0885: c/v = ?; E from H in a medium; wavelength of 3 GHz in a dielectric of ε_r = 2.25; μ_r = ε_r = 2: v = ?.

Asked in: 2019 (1), 2021 (3), 2022 (2), 2024 (1), 2025 (1)

5. The few genuinely new questions

These do not fit a repeating pattern. See each once; they do not need heavy practice.

Two plane polarised waves with a phase difference of π/3 and equal amplitudes combine: the amplitude of the resultant, 2E₀cos(π/6) = 1.7E₀ (a superposition question, not specific to EM waves) (2025)

Two infinite straight wires at right angles with equal currents (one towards, one away from the origin): the magnetic field at a point P(x, y); this is a magnetic-effect-of-current question that the book prints in this chapter (2021)

6. Suggested study order for students

Start with the fields of the wave (Type 1, 32 questions, 28%): E × B for directions, E₀ = cB₀ for amplitudes and the field equation. Add the amplitude questions (Type 5, 10).

Then intensity and energy (Type 2, 20 questions, 18%): I = ½ε₀cE₀² and the equal sharing of energy between the fields.

Then the spectrum (Type 3, 17 questions): learn the order of the bands, their uses and sources; these are the quickest marks of the chapter.

Finish with Maxwell's equations and statements (Type 4, 15), the force and pressure of light (Type 6, 10), the wave in a medium (Type 7, 8) and the two one-off questions.

7. A routine to follow for every question

Decide first which family the question belongs to: directions and vector equations, intensity and energy, the spectrum, Maxwell equations and statements, or the force and pressure of light.

For a field question find the magnitude with E₀ = cB₀ (use the speed of the wave if it is not c) and the direction from E × B along the direction of travel; check the sign of the phase.

For intensity and energy write I = ½ε₀cE₀² or u = ½ε₀E₀² first; for a source use I = P/(4πr²) with the radiated power only.

For the spectrum learn the order of the bands by wavelength; a matching question is usually solved by the order alone.

For radiation pressure ask whether the surface absorbs or reflects (I/c or 2I/c) and whether the light is in a medium.
