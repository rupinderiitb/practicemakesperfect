Types of questions asked in JEE Main 2019–2025 (181 questions)

Key message: these 181 questions fall into only 8 repeating types. The three biggest types (Types 1, 2, 3) account for 89 questions (49%). The f-block and magnetic-moment types (1 and 2) are 64 questions (35%). About 22% of the questions ask for a numerical (integer) answer, so there are no options to guess from.

How to read this: a “type” is the formula, law or technique that decides the answer. So every type below tells you what the question looks like and which formula or method to use.

3. Type-by-type detail

Type 1: Lanthanoids and actinoids: configuration, oxidation states, colour and contraction – 34 questions

What is asked: Write an f-block configuration, count f electrons, decide which Ln ion is an oxidant or reductant, or judge colour, magnetism and contraction.

What to master: Ln: [Xe]4fⁿ6s²; stable f⁰, f⁷, f¹⁴ ions; Ce⁴⁺ and Tb⁴⁺ oxidants, Eu²⁺ and Yb²⁺ reductants; lanthanoid contraction.

Forms that appear: Es and Np configurations, Nd²⁺, Gd²⁺, Ho³⁺, diamagnetic and colourless Ln ions, Ce⁴⁺ and Eu²⁺ strength, mischmetal, Pu removal, Bk³⁺ against Np³⁺.

Asked in: 2019 (4), 2020 (3), 2021 (10), 2022 (4), 2023 (4), 2024 (7), 2025 (2)

Which form was asked:

Electronic configuration of lanthanoid and actinoid atoms and ions; number of f electrons: 10 questions

Oxidation states and oxidising or reducing behaviour of Ln ions: 13 questions

Colour, magnetism, lanthanoid contraction, actinoids and general facts: 11 questions

Type 2: Spin-only magnetic moment and unpaired electrons – 30 questions

What is asked: Find the number of unpaired electrons or the spin-only moment of an ion, or the species identified from a property.

What to master: μ = √[n(n+2)] BM; d configuration of M²⁺ and M³⁺; identify the species from the property first.

Forms that appear: V²⁺ and Co²⁺ pairs, Fe in the ground state, [Ni(NH₃)₆]²⁺, Cr oxides, Mn₂O₃ and MnF₄, Co³⁺ as oxidant, MO₄²⁻ with M of least radius.

Asked in: 2019 (2), 2020 (1), 2021 (1), 2022 (7), 2023 (1), 2024 (12), 2025 (6)

Which form was asked:

Unpaired electrons and μ of given ions: 13 questions

μ of an ion identified from a property (oxidising power, basic oxide, enthalpy of atomisation): 11 questions

μ of the products of KMnO₄, K₂MnO₄ and chromite reactions: 6 questions

Type 3: Compounds, alloys, catalysts and qualitative analysis – 25 questions

What is asked: Name an alloy, catalyst or compound, or identify a salt or gas from a test.

What to master: Composition of brass, bronze, German silver; Nessler's reagent; Mohr's salt; CuI + I₂; ring test and acetate test.

Forms that appear: Gun metal, German silver, Lindlar catalyst, K₂HgI₄, Fe₄[Fe(CN)₆]₃, CuSO₄·5H₂O, Ni(dmg)₂, aqua regia and cobalt nitrite test.

Asked in: 2019 (3), 2020 (2), 2021 (4), 2022 (1), 2023 (7), 2024 (4), 2025 (4)

Which form was asked:

Alloys, catalysts, interstitial compounds and general metal properties: 10 questions

Qualitative analysis and mixed problems: 4 questions

Type 4: KMnO₄ and manganate: preparation, structure and oxidising action – 24 questions

What is asked: Give the product of KMnO₄ in a given medium, the change in oxidation number, or a fact about KMnO₄ or K₂MnO₄.

What to master: MnO₄⁻ → Mn²⁺ (acid), MnO₂ (neutral), MnO₄²⁻ (strong alkali); K₂MnO₄ disproportionates in acid.

Forms that appear: MnO₂ fusion with KOH, heating at 513 K, MnO₄⁻ with I⁻, S₂O₃²⁻ and H₂O₂, oxalate titration, Mn₂O₇ structure.

Asked in: 2019 (2), 2020 (1), 2021 (3), 2022 (8), 2023 (4), 2024 (4), 2025 (2)

Which form was asked:

Preparation, structure and properties of KMnO₄, K₂MnO₄ and Mn₂O₇: 10 questions

Oxidation by MnO₄⁻ in acidic, neutral and alkaline medium: 10 questions

Titration with oxalate and electron change: 4 questions

Type 5: Trends in the 3d series: ionisation enthalpy, atomisation, E° and statements – 20 questions

What is asked: Order or choose 3d metals by ionisation enthalpy, enthalpy of atomisation, density, E° or hydration enthalpy, or judge statements on group trends.

What to master: IE₃(Mn) highest; ΔatomH(V) highest and Zn lowest; E°(Cu²⁺/Cu) positive; Mn³⁺ strongest oxidant; Zn, Cd, Hg properties.

Forms that appear: IE order Ti, Mn, Ni, Zn, Mn²⁺ against Fe²⁺, density order, M²⁺/M potentials, Cr²⁺ and V²⁺ with acids, Mo and W against Cr.

Asked in: 2019 (3), 2020 (2), 2021 (2), 2022 (4), 2023 (1), 2024 (5), 2025 (3)

Type 6: Chromium: K₂Cr₂O₇, chromates and chromyl chloride – 19 questions

What is asked: Give the product, oxidation state, colour or structure in a dichromate or chromyl chloride reaction.

What to master: Cr₂O₇²⁻ ⇌ CrO₄²⁻; Cr(VI) → Cr(III) is green; CrO₂Cl₂ test; CrO₅ is blue.

Forms that appear: chromite fusion, K₂Cr₂O₇ with H₂S and SO₂, chromyl chloride with NaOH and H₂O₂, dichromate structure and Cr–O bonds.

Asked in: 2020 (1), 2021 (1), 2022 (2), 2023 (7), 2024 (5), 2025 (3)

5. Suggested study order for students

Start with the f-block (Type 1): 34 questions (19%) and the magnetic moment (Type 2): 30 questions (17%). Learn the configurations of Ln ions and μ = √[n(n+2)].

Then the reactions of KMnO₄ and K₂Cr₂O₇ (Types 4 and 6): 43 questions (24%). Memorise the products in acidic, neutral and alkaline medium and the chromyl chloride test.

Then compounds and analysis, trends, configuration and oxidation states, colour and oxides (Types 3, 5, 7, 8): 74 questions (41%).

6. A routine to follow for every question

Write the element and its atomic number, then the oxidation state asked for.

Write the d (or f) configuration of the ion: remove the s electrons first.

Count the unpaired electrons and, if asked, μ = √[n(n+2)] BM.

For a reaction, write the medium (acidic, neutral, alkaline) and the products from NCERT.

Check the answer against exceptions: Cr, Cu, Eu, Gd, Ce, and the colour of d⁰ and d¹⁰ ions.
