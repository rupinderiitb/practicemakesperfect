Key message: these 149 questions fall into only 7 repeating types. The three biggest types (Types 1, 2, 3) account for 96 questions (64%). The calculation types (2, 3 and 4) are 75 questions (50%). About 53% of the questions ask for a numerical (integer) answer, so there are no options to guess from.

How to read this: a “type” is the formula, law or technique that decides the answer. So every type below tells you what the question looks like and which formula or method to use.

3. Type-by-type detail

Type 1: Conductance: conductivity, molar conductivity, Kohlrausch's law and electrolytes – 40 questions

What is asked: Calculate κ, Λm, the cell constant, α or Ka, or decide from a graph or statement how conductance changes with concentration.

What to master: κ = G × cell constant; Λm = κ ÷ c; Kohlrausch's law of independent migration; Λm = Λ° − A√c; α = Λm ÷ Λ°.

Forms that appear: 0.8 M electrolyte of resistivity 5×10⁻³ Ω cm, KCl cell constant, mercury cell, BaCl₂, H₂SO₄ and HCl, NH₄OH, acetic acid, AgBr with AgNO₃, Λm against √c graphs for KCl, NaCl, A and B, conductometric titration.

Asked in: 2019 (3), 2020 (4), 2021 (8), 2022 (6), 2023 (5), 2024 (7), 2025 (7)

Which form was asked:

Strong and weak electrolytes: Λm against √c, graphs and statements: 12 questions

Factors, units, ionic conductance and Kohlrausch statements: 9 questions

Conductivity, cell constant and molar conductivity from data: 9 questions

Kohlrausch's law, degree of dissociation and Ka of a weak electrolyte: 8 questions

Conductivity of a sparingly soluble salt and of a mixture: 2 questions

Type 2: Nernst equation: cell and half-cell potentials – 31 questions

What is asked: Find the cell potential, a concentration, pH, pressure or the number of electrons from the Nernst equation.

What to master: E = E° − (0.059/n) log Q; Q from the cell reaction; [H⁺] from pH; Ksp for hydroxides.

Forms that appear: Zn|Zn²⁺‖Sn^x+|Sn, Cu|Cu²⁺‖Ag⁺|Ag, Pt|H₂|H⁺‖Cu²⁺|Cu, Fe³⁺/Fe²⁺ with H₂, MnO₄⁻ at different pH, Cr₂O₇²⁻, Cu(OH)₂, quinhydrone electrode, photoelectric cell.

Asked in: 2019 (1), 2020 (3), 2021 (7), 2022 (5), 2023 (7), 2024 (4), 2025 (4)

Which form was asked:

Cell potential from the Nernst equation with given concentrations: 15 questions

Half-cell potential: effect of pH, pressure and Ksp: 12 questions

Concentration cells and combined problems (quinhydrone, photoelectric, HNO₃): 4 questions

Type 3: Standard electrode potential, E°cell, ΔG° and equilibrium constant – 25 questions

What is asked: Find E°cell, ΔG°, K or the potential of a half-reaction from other half-reactions.

What to master: E°cell = E°cathode − E°anode; ΔG° = −nFE°; log K = nE°/0.059; potentials combine through ΔG.

Forms that appear: Cu, Ag, Zn, Mg half-cells, Fe³⁺/Fe²⁺ and Fe²⁺/Fe, FeO₄²⁻ ladder, Sn⁴⁺/Sn²⁺, Pb⁴⁺/Pb²⁺, Cu²⁺/Cu⁺, Cd–Hg₂SO₄ cell.

Asked in: 2019 (6), 2020 (5), 2021 (2), 2022 (3), 2023 (4), 2024 (2), 2025 (3)

Which form was asked:

E°cell, spontaneity and ΔG° = −nFE°: 8 questions

Equilibrium constant: log K = nE°/0.059: 7 questions

Combining half-reactions: potentials that are not additive: 8 questions

Entropy and enthalpy from the temperature coefficient: 2 questions

Type 4: Faraday's laws of electrolysis: mass, charge, time and gas volume – 19 questions

What is asked: Find the mass or volume liberated, or the charge, current or time needed, for a given electrolysis.

What to master: m = (M/n)(It/F); 1 F = 96500 C; electrons per mole of product; current efficiency.

Forms that appear: Ag, Zn, Al, Ni, Au, Fe and Cu deposited, O₂ and H₂ from water, Cr₂O₇²⁻, MnO₄⁻, Fe₃O₄, KClO₃ from KCl, NaCl and pH.

Asked in: 2019 (1), 2020 (3), 2021 (2), 2022 (4), 2023 (1), 2024 (6), 2025 (2)

Which form was asked:

Mass or volume deposited or evolved by a given charge: 9 questions

Charge or time needed, and efficiency of electrolysis: 10 questions

Type 5: Commercial cells, batteries, fuel cells and corrosion – 14 questions

What is asked: Name the electrodes, reactions or uses of a dry cell, lead storage battery or fuel cell, or judge statements on corrosion.

What to master: Electrode reactions of each cell; charging and discharging; rusting as an electrochemical cell.

Forms that appear: Leclanché, Ni–Cd, mercury and fuel cells, lead storage battery charging, methanol fuel cell, calomel electrode, rusting of iron.

Asked in: 2019 (1), 2022 (2), 2023 (1), 2024 (6), 2025 (4)

Which form was asked:

Primary and secondary cells, lead storage battery and fuel cells: 10 questions

Reference electrodes and corrosion: 4 questions

Type 6: Electrochemical series: oxidising and reducing power and cell notation – 13 questions

What is asked: Order species by oxidising or reducing power, decide which metals a reagent oxidises, or write a cell for a reaction.

What to master: Higher E° = stronger oxidising agent; spontaneous reaction when E°cell > 0; cell notation.

Forms that appear: ClO₄⁻, IO₄⁻ and BrO₄⁻, M³⁺/M²⁺ for V, Cr, Mn and Co, Cl₂, I₂, Ag⁺, Na⁺ and Li⁺, metals oxidised by Cr₂O₇²⁻ and NO₃⁻.

Asked in: 2019 (1), 2021 (1), 2022 (1), 2023 (5), 2024 (3), 2025 (2)

Which form was asked:

Oxidising and reducing power from standard potentials: 8 questions

Cell notation and statements on electrode potential: 5 questions

Type 7: Electrolysis: products at the electrodes and electrolytic cells – 7 questions

What is asked: Decide what is formed at the anode or cathode, which metal deposits first, or when a galvanic cell becomes electrolytic.

What to master: Standard potentials decide discharge; inert and active electrodes; external voltage against E°cell.

Forms that appear: brine, AgNO₃ with Pt and Ag electrodes, dilute and concentrated H₂SO₄, Cu²⁺, Ag⁺, Hg₂²⁺ and Mg²⁺ mixture, Zn–Cu cell with an external voltage.

Asked in: 2020 (2), 2023 (2), 2024 (1), 2025 (2)

5. Suggested study order for students

Start with conductance (Type 1): 40 questions (27%). Learn κ, Λm, Kohlrausch's law and the shape of the Λm against √c graphs.

Then the Nernst equation and standard potentials (Types 2 and 3): 56 questions (38%). One formula, E = E° − (0.059/n) log Q, and ΔG° = −nFE° cover most of them.

Then Faraday's laws, batteries, the electrochemical series and electrolysis products (Types 4, 5, 6, 7): 53 questions (36%).

6. A routine to follow for every question

Write what is given and what is asked, with the units (κ in S m⁻¹ or S cm⁻¹, F = 96500 C mol⁻¹).

Decide the concept: Nernst equation, E°cell and ΔG°, Faraday's law or conductance.

Write the cell reaction or the half-reaction and find n, the electrons transferred.

Apply the formula once: E = E° − (0.059/n) log Q, ΔG° = −nFE°, m = (M/n)(It/F), Λm = κ/c.

Check the answer: sign of E, units, and the rounding asked (nearest integer).
