Arrhenius and first order types (1 and 2) are 79 questions (53%). About 59% of the questions ask for a numerical (integer) answer, so there are no options to guess from.

How to read this: a “type” is the formula, law or technique that decides the answer. So every type below tells you what the question looks like and which formula or method to use.

3. Type-by-type detail

Type 1: Arrhenius equation, activation energy, catalysts, energy profiles and mechanisms – 42 questions

What is asked: Find Eₐ or k at another temperature, read a plot or equation of ln k against 1/T, judge the effect of a catalyst on Eₐ, or find the rate-determining step and intermediates from a mechanism or energy diagram.

What to master: ln(k₂/k₁) = (Eₐ/R)(1/T₁ − 1/T₂); slope = −Eₐ/R; catalyst lowers Eₐ forward and backward equally; ΔH = Eₐf − Eₐb; slow step fixes the rate law.

Forms that appear: Eₐ from k at two temperatures, k = Ae^(−Eₐ/RT) equations, Eₐ of k₁k₂/k₃, catalysed Eₐ, energy diagrams with three steps, NO + Br₂ mechanism.

Asked in: 2019 (4), 2020 (8), 2021 (7), 2022 (7), 2023 (7), 2024 (2), 2025 (7)

Which form was asked:

Activation energy or rate constant from two temperatures: 11 questions

Arrhenius equation, plots of ln k against 1/T and statements: 14 questions

Activation energy of catalysed, reversible and multistep reactions: 6 questions

Energy profiles, intermediates and reaction mechanisms: 11 questions

Type 2: First order reactions: rate constant, half life and time of completion – 37 questions

What is asked: Find k, t½, the time for a given percentage of completion or the fraction left, including radioactive decay and drug problems.

What to master: k = (2.303/t) log([A]₀/[A]); t½ = 0.693/k; fraction left = (½)^(t/t½).

Forms that appear: 99.9% against t½, 90% from 50% data, azomethane decomposition, A → 2B, radioactive tracer, carbon dating, drug potency.

Asked in: 2020 (2), 2021 (10), 2022 (8), 2023 (6), 2024 (7), 2025 (4)

Which form was asked:

Time for a given percentage of completion: 13 questions

Rate constant, half life and concentration from given data: 14 questions

Radioactive decay, drug and carbon-dating problems: 10 questions

Type 3: Order and rate law from initial-rate data – 18 questions

What is asked: Find the order of a reaction, the missing entry of a table or k from initial-rate data, or the change in rate when concentration or volume changes.

What to master: Compare rows with one concentration changing; (rate₂/rate₁) = (c₂/c₁)ⁿ; overall order = sum of orders.

Forms that appear: Initial rate tables for 2A + B, NO + Cl₂, 2NO + H₂, missing X and Y, doubling concentration, volume reduction, elementary reactions.

Asked in: 2019 (3), 2020 (1), 2021 (3), 2022 (2), 2023 (1), 2024 (6), 2025 (2)

Which form was asked:

Order and rate constant from initial-rate tables: 11 questions

Effect of changing concentration or volume on rate; rate-law statements: 7 questions

Type 4: Graphs of concentration, rate and rate constant – 15 questions

What is asked: Choose the graph for a zero or first order reaction, read order from plots, or use growth and decay curves.

What to master: [A] against t: zero order straight; ln[A] against t: first order straight; rate against [A] gives the order.

Forms that appear: Zero order graph, equilibrium rate against time, bacterial growth and decay, k against T plots, log k against 1/T for three reactions.

Asked in: 2019 (3), 2020 (3), 2021 (2), 2023 (2), 2025 (5)

Which form was asked:

Concentration-time and rate graphs of zero and first order reactions: 8 questions

Growth and decay curves: 3 questions

Plots of k against T and of log k against 1/T: 4 questions

Type 5: Zero order reactions, half-life dependence and units of k – 13 questions

What is asked: Use the zero-order law, find the order from the dependence of t½ on [A]₀, or give the unit of k.

What to master: [A] = [A]₀ − kt; t½ ∝ [A]₀^(1−n); unit of k = (mol L⁻¹)^(1−n) time⁻¹.

Forms that appear: A → B with t½ = 50 min, t½ against pressure, decomposition of AB₃, unit of k for order n, statements on half lives.

Asked in: 2019 (2), 2021 (1), 2022 (3), 2023 (4), 2025 (3)

Which form was asked:

Zero order reactions: rate constant, half life and concentration: 5 questions

Order from the dependence of half life on concentration: 5 questions

Units of k and statements on order: 3 questions

Type 6: Parallel first order decays and the steady-state approximation – 10 questions

What is asked: Find the time at which two decaying species reach a given ratio, or the concentration of an intermediate or an equilibrium constant relation.

What to master: Each decay: [X] = [X]₀(½)^(t/t½); steady state: d[B]/dt = 0; K = k_f/k_b.

Forms that appear: A and B with t½ 300 s and 180 s, A and B with 54 and 18 min, two independent decays, A → B → C, A₂ ⇌ 2A.

Asked in: 2019 (3), 2020 (1), 2021 (1), 2022 (1), 2023 (2), 2024 (1), 2025 (1)

Which form was asked:

Parallel and competing first order decays: 6 questions

Steady-state approximation and reversible steps: 4 questions

Type 7: Rate of reaction and rates of formation or disappearance – 8 questions

What is asked: Relate the rates of change of different species to the reaction rate, or find coefficients from the rates.

What to master: Rate = −(1/a)d[A]/dt = (1/c)d[C]/dt.

Forms that appear: KClO₃ + FeSO₄ + H₂SO₄ rate of Fe₂(SO₄)₃, 2N₂O₅ → 4NO₂ + O₂, breath analyser, 2A + 3B → 3P, unknown coefficients.

Asked in: 2019 (2), 2020 (1), 2021 (2), 2022 (1), 2023 (1), 2024 (1)

Which form was asked:

Rate of reaction and of formation or disappearance of species: 6 questions

Stoichiometric coefficients from rates: 2 questions

Type 8: Total-pressure analysis of first order gas reactions – 7 questions

What is asked: Find k or the pressure of A from the total pressure at time t and at infinity for A(g) → B(g) + C(g) and similar.

What to master: Pₐ = Pᵢ − x with Pₜ = Pᵢ + x (or Pᵢ + 2x); k = (2.303/t) log(Pᵢ/Pₐ).

Forms that appear: A(g) → 2B(g) + C(g), N₂O₅ decomposition at constant volume, expressions for k in Pᵢ and Pₜ, Pₜ and P∞.

Asked in: 2023 (1), 2024 (3), 2025 (3)

Which form was asked:

First order gas-phase reactions followed by total pressure: 7 questions

5. Suggested study order for students

Start with the Arrhenius equation and activation energy (Type 1): 42 questions (28%) and first order reactions (Type 2): 37 questions (25%). Learn k = (2.303/t) log([A]₀/[A]) and ln(k₂/k₁) = (Eₐ/R)(1/T₁ − 1/T₂).

Then rate laws, graphs and zero order (Types 3, 4 and 5): 46 questions (31%). Practise finding order from tables and half-life dependence.

Then parallel decays, rates of formation and total-pressure problems (Types 6, 7, 8): 25 questions (17%).

6. A routine to follow for every question

Write the rate law or the form of the integrated law for the order given.

List what is known: concentrations, times, temperatures, half life.

Pick the matching formula: first order k = (2.303/t) log([A]₀/[A]); Arrhenius ln(k₂/k₁) = (Eₐ/R)(1/T₁ − 1/T₂).

Check units (s⁻¹, mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹, kJ mol⁻¹, K) before substituting.

Round the numerical value to the nearest integer if the question asks for it.
